      The problem

      A new viewer opens Kuku TV and meets about 30 shows.

      Onboarding asks only the language. Then three scrolls of 25 to 30 posters to guess from.

      Why it hurts

      They are asked to pay before any story hooks them.

      Every plan auto-renews, with no free trial shown. Price and billing come up in 20% of 500 Kuku TV English Play Store reviews.

      1. 5 questionsin chat

      The fix: Kuku Daily

      Two shows, one short drop a day, and a timer that brings you back.

      A drop of about 4 minutes ends on a cliffhanger, and the next unlocks at your free time.

      Subscribers wait less. Nobody binges.
