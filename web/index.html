A concept for Kuku TV. Five questions in chat, two shows picked for you, one short drop a day (about 4 minutes) that stops on a cliffhanger, and the next drop unlocks at the hour you said you are free.

  • 25 to 30 showsThree scrolls show about 8 rows of posters to choose from.
  • 20% of reviewsPrice and billing come up in 20% of 500 Kuku TV English Play Store reviews, and in about half of the 1 and 2 star ones. Plans run Rs 199 a month to Rs 1,499 a year, auto-renew, with no trial shown.

    How it works

    The model gets two jobs: reading the viewer and reading the scripts. Everything that has to be predictable, like filters, drop sizes, quote checks, timers and streaks, is plain code.

    1. 1

      Screenplay

      Each episode as scenes, lines, speakers, cues and timings.

      Input

    2. 2

      Score each ending 1 to 5

      The model rates the last 20 seconds. Rules score the same lines.

      AICode

    3. 3

      Pack 3.5 to 6 minute drops

      Two or three episodes per drop, cut at the strongest cliffhanger nearby.

      Code

    4. 4

      Check the teaser

      The teaser must quote a real line from the ending. If it does not, code repairs it.

      AICode

    5. 5

      Timer gates

      Waits by plan and streak, reminders in your free slot, streaks that fall one level.

    What the real run produced

    One run of the pipeline over the 50 episode scripts from the assignment.

      Where it fails

      • Gibberish answers still get two generic shows. It should ask again instead.

      • Scores read only the script. A slap the camera holds on scores like a calm line.

      • Teasers lean on stock phrases. Code checks the quoted line, not the line around it.

      • Retention gains are untested on real viewers. This is a concept and a working pipeline, not a measured result.
