# Micro-drama research (for promo-finder and AI microdrama problem)

Compiled 2026-11-07 from web search and two page fetches. Source dates are given where the page showed one; otherwise "date not shown". Vendor blogs are marked as such. Nothing here was checked against a primary filing or the apps themselves.

## 1. What micro-dramas are, origin, market

- Vertical, serialized dramas, episodes about 1 to 2 minutes, built for phones. Roots: serialized sketches and relationship dramas on Douyin, Kuaishou and Bilibili from about 2018; China's regulator recognised microdrama as its own online video category in 2020 (Wikipedia mirror, 2026-03 snapshot: https://wiki.krisyotam.com/content/wikipedia_en_all_nopic_2026-03/Microdrama). Hongguo/Douyin role not confirmed [unverified].
- ReelShort launched August 2022 from Crazy Maple Studio and passed $100M cumulative in-app revenue in 2023 (Vitrina, date not shown: https://vitrina.ai/?p=158395) [vendor blog].
- Market size varies by scope: Omdia $11B global revenue in 2025, $14B by end 2026 (via Vitrina, https://vitrina.ai/blog/micro-drama-distribution-platforms-2026/); Sensor Tower app-store IAP only $2.98B in 2025 (https://sensortower.com/blog/state-of-short-drama-apps-2025). Do not mix the two.
- Downloads: 2.3B in 2025, more than double 2024; 370M in Q1 2025, 6.2x YoY (Sensor Tower, same link). Q1 2026 over 850M, +140% YoY (secondary report, Sensor Tower attribution) [unverified].
- US attention: microdrama apps passed Netflix and Prime Video in US mobile engagement (eMarketer, date not shown: https://www.emarketer.com/content/microdrama-apps-surpass-netflix--prime-video-us-mobile-engagement).
- India: market above $300M, 450M downloads, 100M MAU in 2025 (Lumikai via Entrepreneur India, https://india.entrepreneur.com/technology/indias-usd-300-mn-microdrama-market-is-just-taking-off). Analysts call it an "exploratory phase" (MPA, via exchange4media).

## 2. Main apps

- Leaders by revenue: ReelShort and DramaBox. Q1 2025 IAP: ReelShort $130M (+31%), DramaBox $120M (+29%); cumulative to March 2025 $490M and $450M (Sensor Tower via secondary summary, https://vitrina.ai/blog/micro-drama-distribution-platforms-2026/). Q1 2026 each near $140M (single secondary source) [unverified].
- Others: ShortMax, GoodShort, FlexTV, MoboReels, FlickReels, DramaWave/DramaRush, Fun Drama (ad-intelligence profiles at https://blog.insightrackr.com/en/docs/ReelShort-Mobile-Advertising-Intelligence-Analysis, date not shown).
- India: Kuku TV (launched Feb 2025, up to 2 min episodes, 50+ episodes, Hindi/Telugu/Kannada/Bangla; https://www.afaqs.com/news/ott-streaming/kuku-fm-launches-vertical-microdrama-ott-platform-kuku-tv-8718086). Claimed 170M downloads and #1 Play Store microdrama rank, about Rs 899 per year subscription, Krafton funding up to $85M (aggregated report, date not shown) [unverified]. Rivals: QuickTV (ShareChat), Story TV, Reelies, DashReels, JioHotstar's Tadka.
- Who leads: ReelShort and DramaBox globally; Kuku TV in India by downloads (claimed).

## 3. Monetization

- Free first episodes then paywall. Free count is quoted anywhere from 5 to 15; production guides suggest paywall after episode 5 to 10 (Vitrina production guide, https://vitrina.ai/blog/micro-drama-production-guide/) [vendor blog].
- Coins unlock episodes; prices quoted as $0.10 to $0.20 per episode in one source and $0.50 to $1.00 in another, bundles $9.99 / $19.99 / $49.99 (RevenueCat and Spyro-Soft blogs, dates not shown). Treat as [unverified]; check in-app.
- Subscriptions (weekly/annual) and rewarded ads coexist; ReelShort lets users earn up to 175 coins by watching ads (blog roundup) [unverified].
- UA spend: ReelShort reported loss-making on heavy marketing; DramaBox 2024 revenue $323M, net profit $10M (secondary) [unverified].

## 4. Format and tropes

- 60 to 100 episodes (some 200+) at 60 to 90 s each (Vitrina production guide). Kuku TV: up to 2 min, 50+ episodes.
- Pacing (industry practice, no single source): shock or conflict in first seconds, a turn every episode, every episode ends on a cliffhanger, big reversal near the paywall episode.
- Genres and tropes: billionaire/CEO romance, revenge, hidden identity, contract or forced marriage, werewolf and romantasy, mafia, Dragon King, regency. AppGrowing ad review of 500+ creatives, 2026-06-02 to 07-02 (published 2026-07-02): identity reversal, contract/forced marriage, public humiliation and revenge, forbidden romance, zero-cost access (https://appgrowing.net/blog/en/how-to-find-high-priority-short-drama-app-ad-hooks-from-competitor-data/).

## 5. AI-generated microdramas

- Volume: over 10,000 AI titles a month in China by early 2026 (VML, https://www.vml.com/insight/ai-micro-dramas) [unverified].
- Cost: under 5,000 yuan per AI-assisted episode in 3 to 7 days versus 50,000 to 100,000 yuan and 2 to 4 weeks live action (Pulse, https://www.pulse.bot/television/news/microdramas-the-next-big-leap-a3b00e6d-7de8-43ae-aab6-9b1b4587bc85/); Judian 100k to 150k yuan per drama, about a third of live action; one Western tool-vendor case about $1,000 per episode (Invideo, https://invideo.io/blog/ai-micro-drama-cost/) [vendor]. Live action about $150k to $300k per season (TheWrap, Oct 2026, https://www.thewrap.com/vertical-short-dramas-industry-explained-8-billion-business/).
- Quality issues: flat emotion in key scenes, inconsistent tone between episodes, lip-sync and robotic voice, hallucinated plot points and shifting motivations (1stopasia, https://www.1stopasia.com/blog/wp-content/uploads/articles-download/The-AI-voiceover-problem-in-Chinese-drama-and-what-it-actually-costs.pdf; singularitymoments.com) [vendor/opinion]. No independent audience study found.
- Localization: AI dubbing and subtitle pipelines for Chinese-to-English (Morphic, JollyToday, Polilingua; all vendors). Hybrid human review recommended.

## 6. Promos and ads

Hard evidence is thin; most is third-party model estimates.
- DramaBox US, 2026-07-01 to 08-14 (AdMapix, 2026-08-22, https://www.admapix.com/blog/ad-intelligence/dramabox-ad-strategy-2026): top 10 ads run 46 to 116 s. Seven ads of 46 to 64 s hold 22.2% of estimated impressions; three of 111 to 116 s hold 77.8%; one 111 s Dragon King romance alone 70.5% of 298.1M estimated impressions. Top 10 are 7 unique videos. Scale one proven hook, test premises around it. Ad themes: parent humiliated defending child (54 s), ex betrayal (55 s), hidden-power janitor revenge (46 s), heiress in fake relationship (54 s). Text overlays, CTA and footage source were NOT described.
- SocialPeta (2026-06, https://gameindustrylibrary.com/documents/from-scale-to-sustainability-how-short-drama-app-marketing-will-be-redefined-in-2026): formula is hook in first 3 s, intense conflict, highlight clips, cliffhanger ending [vendor].
- AppGrowing: interactive "choice question" opening in first 3 s gave the lowest CPI in SEA (https://appgrowing.net/blog/en/ci-games-and-short-drama-ua-strategy-the-creative-cross-over-opportunity/, date not shown) [unverified].
- Creative volume: DramaBox ads +22.3%, creatives +13.7%; ReelShort ads +19.3%, creatives -22.6% (AppGrowing, June to July 2026). Hawky: DramaBox ~300 Meta ads Aug 2024 to Nov 2025, 94% still live (https://hawky.ai/ad-gallery/drama-box-us), with model-assigned labels.
- Promos are cut from the show's own scenes (common practice, no direct source) [unverified]. Stitched montage vs single scene: not established by any source found.

## 7. Hook taxonomy (working list, synthesized; see AppGrowing for the sourced five)

Hooks: slap/physical shock; public humiliation; identity reveal; betrayal at a wedding or altar; pregnancy or secret-child reveal; threat or kidnap; rich-reveal (poor-looking lead is a billionaire); contract or forced marriage offer; forbidden romance; death/rebirth; choice question to viewer.
Cliffhangers: mid-reveal cut (face turns, name about to be said); threat unresolved; door opens on a stranger; confession interrupted; twist line of dialogue; cut on a physical strike before impact.

## 8. Retention and success metrics

No public numbers found for completion rate, episode 1 to 10 drop-off, paywall conversion, ROAS or ad CTR. Only third-party claims that the first-3-second hook and end cliffhanger lift CTR and conversion, with no values. AdMapix and AppGrowing both state they lack CTR/CPI per hook.
