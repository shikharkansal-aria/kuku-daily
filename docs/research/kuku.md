## 2. Products
- Kuku FM: audio series, audiobooks, stories; iOS listing "Kuku FM: Audiobooks & Stories" (https://apps.apple.com/au/app/id1551919780). Courses [unverified].
- Kuku TV: vertical microdrama app launched late 2024 (https://www.afaqs.com/news/ott-streaming/kuku-fm-launches-vertical-microdrama-ott-platform-kuku-tv-8718086). Separate US brand: [unverified].

Other markets: weekly/annual plans, e.g. Malaysia RM49.90 weekly (App Store MY listing).
- Pay per episode / coins: [unverified] (common in rivals).
- Marketing: Meta ads drive acquisition; "1.6x higher ROAS, 29% lower cost per subscription" (https://www.medianews4u.com/kuku-tv-sees-1-6x-hhgher-roas-29-lower-cost-per-subscription-with-metas-advantage-report/, 2026-10).

## 7. Competitors (from general knowledge, [unverified], no sources fetched)
- Pocket FM (US apps incl. Pocket Drama): larger audio base, AI-heavy writing; Kuku leans harder into video and live action.
- Pratilipi: written and audio stories; Kuku is video-first now.
- STAGE: regional-language OTT with Haryanvi/Rajasthani focus; Kuku covers more languages.
- ReelShort, DramaBox: global Chinese-backed microdrama leaders; Kuku is India-first with Indian languages.
- Story TV: [unverified]; small player.
